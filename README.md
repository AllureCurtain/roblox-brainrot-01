Meme Tide Tycoon — a 3D Roblox brainrot game: collect absurd meme sea creatures as they ride the conveyor, keep them on your island base, and earn cash every second. Climb the rarity ladder from Soggy Sockfish to The Infinite Sandwich, mutate them Golden or Rainbow, rebirth for a permanent income multiplier, and defend your base with a lock — or steal from others while their lock is down. Every ~90 seconds a tsunami sweeps the island: get back inside your base or be washed back to spawn.

## How to play

- **Collect:** Brainrots ride the conveyor across the island. Walk up to one and press the Buy prompt — it costs 10× its $/sec and walks to a slot on your base.
- **Earn:** Every brainrot on your base pays its $/sec into your cash, once a second. Rarer brainrots pay far more: Soggy Sockfish $1/s, Grumpy Toaster $2/s, Captain Pickle $6/s, DJ Llama $10/s, Sir Barksalot $25/s, Noodle Ninja $40/s, Quantum Quokka $100/s, Emperor Penguin CEO $150/s, Galactic Garbage Goblin $400/s, The Infinite Sandwich $1000/s.
- **Mutate:** The Shop rolls a mutation for one of your brainrots — Golden (×2), Rainbow (×5), or Frozen (×1.5).
- **Upgrade:** The Shop also sells Walk Speed and extra base slots (up to 8).
- **Rebirth:** At $10,000 cash (or while owning a Mythic), reset your cash and brainrots for a permanent ×2 income multiplier per rebirth, up to ×32 at 5 rebirths.
- **Steal:** Walk up to a brainrot on another player's base while their lock is off and press Steal — it follows you home. If the owner touches you while you carry it, it goes straight back. Lock your own base for 60s (120s cooldown) to stop thieves.
- **Survive:** Every ~90 seconds a tsunami warning sounds. You have 10 seconds to get inside your own base before the wave sweeps the island — anyone caught outside is washed back to spawn and drops whatever they were carrying.

## Run it

```sh
rojo build default.project.json --output game.rbxl
```

Then open game.rbxl in Roblox Studio and press Play. (Screenshots in screenshots/ are starter placeholders until the owner takes real Studio captures.)
